Clear space

Anatomy, top to bottom

  • Header bandDeep blue background, ~28% of page height, with layered translucent diamond shapes bleeding from the top-right corner.
  • Logo, whiteTop-left, consistent margin from both edges.
  • Headline + subheadBold white H1, then one or two lighter-weight lines below — sometimes italic when it's a tagline instead of a description.
  • Diamond photo frameOne rotated, rounded-corner photo, top-right, straddling the line between the header band and the white body.
  • Two-column introEvery single piece splits its opening copy into two columns with a thin dotted vertical divider — this is the most consistent rule found.
  • Content moduleStat pills, a feature grid, or a comparison/pricing table — whichever fits the piece.
  • Footer bandSame deep blue, small white logo left, phone & email right.
